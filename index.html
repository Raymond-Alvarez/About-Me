Top 10 Signs I’ve Coded Too Long

  1. I try to "Undo" (Ctrl+Z) a mistake made with a real pen.
  2. I start talking to my rubber duck... and expect an answer.
  3. I look at a menu and wonder why it isn't in Monospace.
  4. I see semicolons at the end of every sentence;
  5. I try to 'Save' my progress before leaving the table.
  6. I start counting from zero instead of one (0, 1, 2, 3...).
  7. I look for a "Find" (Ctrl+F) shortcut when I lose my keys in the house.
  8. I've tried to "Comment Out" a conversation I didn't want to have.
  9. I think in `if/else` statements for simple decisions like "Coffee or Tea?"
  10. I realize I’ve been staring at the same bug for so long that I’ve named it.
